07 / MANUELA

Industry perspective.

Manuela Serrano Cegarra is a Venezuelan music business professional, creative strategist, vocalist, arranger, and producer working at the intersection of A&R, artist development, release strategy, marketing, publishing, and live music.

Her experience spans record-label release operations at RIMAS Entertainment, Creative Latin research at BMI, programming and industry research with We Are Moving the Needle, marketing for the Berklee Performance Center, and social-media and community strategy through ComunicARTE.

Across those roles, she has worked with artist research, catalog and publishing intelligence, DSP pitches, release assets, campaign strategy, audience development, artist relations, production logistics, and live-event execution.

Her perspective is shaped by both sides of the work: understanding the artist’s creative process and understanding the systems required to move music into the world. Her focus is especially rooted in Latin music, emerging talent, and building strategies that preserve artistic identity while creating sustainable opportunities.

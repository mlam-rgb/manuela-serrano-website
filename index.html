06 / MANUELA

Creative instinct.
Industry perspective.

Manuela Serrano Cegarra is a Venezuelan music business professional, creative strategist, and musician working across A&R, artist development, marketing, publishing, and international music opportunities.

Her experience includes BMI’s Creative Latin team in New York City, where she worked on artist, songwriter, producer, and catalog research while analyzing industry trends, streaming performance, charts, publishing affiliations, and emerging Latin talent.

Her background also includes work with We Are Moving the Needle, supporting programming and research within the professional music community, and Golden Music, where she worked on international booking strategy, festival research, artist proposals, and connections between musicians and presenters.

Today, Manuela brings these areas together to help artists and creative projects make stronger decisions around identity, positioning, audience, team structure, marketing, and long-term development.
